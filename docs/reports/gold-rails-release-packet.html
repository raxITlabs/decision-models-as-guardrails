Read the provisional numbers with these in mind

  • Jev's 100 on denied topics is against one AI reviewer's labels. A reference drawn by a model may favour systems that reason like it. Treat the size of this gap with more caution than the others.
  • Bedrock's profanity score reflects a definition mismatch. It was scored against our written definition, not AWS's undisclosed managed list. It caught 24% of profane messages and passed 94% of clean ones.
  • Masked spellings are a diagnostic outside the score. On 31 masked spellings such as f*ck, Bedrock flagged 3; Jev flagged 30; the open models flagged 29 to 31.
  • B2 is four pairs. Hard and unresolved pairs were omitted. Bedrock's decision changed on 1 of 4 pairs when only the identity changed; every other system changed on none. That is four individual cases, not a rate.
  • Overall cost is a mix of category checks. It is the mean of the six category costs, each measured separately, with word filters counting both of its checks. It is not the price of one call. No end-to-end request was measured.

Bias stays separate and exploratory. On BBQ questions whose answer is "unknown", Jev and Kev 4B and 9B are right 94 to 100% of the time, while Laya and Open-Jev are right 7 to 11% of the time. The comment and discrim-eval samples are too sparse and too unmatched to rank fairness.

What the check found and fixed

None of these changed a score, interval, cost or threshold.

  • The sign-off path could not reach a clean release. Signing the contract changes its hash, which all three freeze manifests pinned. sign_off.py records the signature together with the matching approvals. It was tested only in a throwaway copy.
  • The masked-spelling diagnostic described its labels as human-reviewed. They come from the single AI reviewer. The file now says so.
  • The overall latency mixed unlike samples and is removed, as described above.
  • The page converter could publish empty data-scope rows from a fresh clone, because release builds are generated files. It now stops and prints the rebuild command.

Limits that stay on the page

  • Intervals cover sampling only, not label errors, source differences or possible training exposure to public datasets.
